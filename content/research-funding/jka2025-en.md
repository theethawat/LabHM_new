# JKA Research Grant (FY2025)

## Current Project

### 2025M-424

- **Project Title**: DX Welfare Care Supporting the 100-Year Life Era: Privacy-Preserving Elderly Monitoring and Health Support System (FY2025)
- **Recipient**: University of Miyazaki
- **Principal Investigator**: Thi Thi Zin (University of Miyazaki, Faculty of Engineering)

## About JKA

The Japan Keirin Autorace Foundation (JKA) is a public interest incorporated foundation that conducts public interest projects such as promotion of mechanical industry, sports promotion, social welfare enhancement, and academic research promotion, funded by proceeds from bicycle racing and auto racing. Through CYCLE JKA Social Action, they broadly develop support activities utilizing subsidies.

## Research Overview

This project aims to develop a privacy-preserving elderly monitoring and health support system to enhance the safety of older adults and reduce the burden on caregivers in Japan's rapidly aging society. By utilizing advanced contactless technologies, the system analyzes elderly behavior and gait information, enabling the detection of abnormal actions and fall risks.

## Contribution to Society

This research contributes to the realization of sustainable welfare care and the improvement of health standards throughout the community, supporting society-wide prevention and health promotion efforts toward the 100-year life era.
