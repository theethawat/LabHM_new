# MIC FORWARD Project

## Current Project

- **Research Topic**: Research and Development of Cattle Dystocia Prediction System Using Heart Rate Estimation with Low-Power Radio Wave Sensors
- **Principal Investigator**: Thi Thi Zin (University of Miyazaki)
- **Division**: Digital Infrastructure Development Division
- **Research Period**: 4 years (planned)

## About FORWARD Project

The Fundamental Technologies for Sustainable Efficient Radio Wave Use R&D Project (FORWARD) is a research and development initiative promoted by the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications. It promotes effective use of radio waves and supports wireless communication technology R&D toward Society 5.0.

## Research Overview

We conduct research and development of a cattle dystocia prediction system using heart rate as an indicator with low-power radio wave sensors. In the Kyushu region, cattle barns are geographically scattered, making rapid intervention during parturition abnormalities difficult. This work integrates heart rate estimation with radio wave sensing, AI-based parturition prediction using image processing, and 3D diagnostic technology using Mixed Reality (MR) devices so veterinarians can provide appropriate midwifery instructions remotely.

## Contribution to Society

This research reduces veterinarian travel burden and decreases parturition accidents due to dystocia. It contributes to solving regional issues and developing a sustainable livestock industry, with the goal of improving livestock competitiveness in the Kyushu region.
